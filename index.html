HOW TO PLAY

  1. Set your bet (1–10 coins) and press DEAL.
  2. Your five cards flip up and are all held. Tap any card you want to discard.
  3. Press DRAW: discards slide away and fresh cards slide in.
  4. The dealer takes his turn, then flips his hand.
  5. Beat his hand to win. A tie returns your bet.

THE CARDS

Every card is a real level from the Extreme Demon List. The #number is its list position, and the coloured tag is its rarity.

HANDS

Cards that share a rarity make a hand, just like Luigi's picture poker. The bigger the match, the more it pays. Winnings = your bet × the multiplier:

If you and the dealer have the same kind of hand, the rarer cards win (see the rarity bar on the left).

THE #1 CARD

The Apex card (#1) wins you the game. Whoever holds more #1 cards wins, and if you both hold the same number it's a draw. You can still discard it if you want to.
